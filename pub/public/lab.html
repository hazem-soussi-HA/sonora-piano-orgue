Every protocol carries a grade. A = mechanism established and replicated · B = the mechanism is real, the effect is small or inconsistent · C = tradition or ritual, no mechanism found · D = the claim is not supported. The ledger states what each protocol does not claim.

Resonance baroreflex · 0.1 Hz

rate 6.0 bpm phase — session 00:00 dose 0 min level -∞ dB
Breathing at the resonance of the baroreflex raises RSA amplitude four- to tenfold and increases baroreflex sensitivity (Lehrer & Gevirtz 2014). Four weeks of training reduced stress, anxiety and depressive symptoms against a control; an individual resonance and a fixed 0.1 Hz performed equally (n=88, 2026). Dose: ≥5 min a day. Not a treatment for any condition.

Entrainment binaural · parameter matters

carrier 340 Hz beat 10 Hz band theta elapsed 00:00 level -∞ dB
The mechanism is real: EEG power at the beat frequency increases during stimulation, and a low carrier (340 Hz) with unmasked beats gives the strongest entrainment (n=80, 2025). The behavioural effect is small and inconsistent: g=0.45 across 22 studies, 5 of 14 EEG studies support entrainment and 8 do not, and the pain evidence is low to very low certainty. Longer exposure, and exposure before the task, work better than exposure during it. Not a treatment.

Somatic interoceptive attention

region — elapsed 00:00 level -∞ dB
Attending to internal sensation raises interoceptive accuracy: 20 of 31 randomised trials (64.5%) show interoception-based interventions beating a non-interoceptive control, strongest in PTSD, IBS, fibromyalgia and substance use — while symptom change stays inconclusive (2023). This is an attention practice, not trauma therapy. The solfege tones are carriers for the practice; they are not the mechanism.

Dose & Safety the budget that matters

No decibel measurement has ever been published for a sound bath. This laboratory measures and shows its own output. ≤70 dBA is unlikely to cause hearing loss even over long exposure; ≥85 dBA with long or repeated exposure can (NIDCD). WHO (2026) weekly limits: 80 dB for 40 h, 90 dB for 4 h, 100 dB for 20 min. Do not use this with epilepsy, tinnitus, hyperacusis, or in pregnancy without medical advice. It is not a substitute for care.
